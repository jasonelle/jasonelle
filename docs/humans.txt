/* HISTORY */

Jasonelle v1 and v2 were forks of Jasonette with improvements, from
2018 to 2022. Jasonette was created by Ethan Gliechtenstein. Its core
began in late 2014 and ended in late 2018.

Jasonelle v3 was created from scratch by Camilo Castro in 2022, a total
rewrite to avoid license issues with the previous core. It was refined
until July 2026.

Jasonelle v4 was built from scratch again, starting in August 2026 and
released in October 2026. A complete rewrite, with better documentation
and better code, and with Android support restored. It breaks
compatibility with the earlier versions. It is built for the era of
agentic programming tools.
