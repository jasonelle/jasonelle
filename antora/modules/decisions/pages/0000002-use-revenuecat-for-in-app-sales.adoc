= Use RevenueCat for managing in-app sales

- id: 0000002

== Status

- Accepted (2026-08-12) by: @clsource

== Context and Problem Statement

Jasonelle apps need to sell digital content through in-app purchases and
subscriptions on both iOS and Android. Each store has its own purchase API,
receipt validation, and subscription lifecycle, which means duplicating a lot of
store-specific logic for every platform.

== Considered Options

* Use https://www.revenuecat.com/[RevenueCat] for managing in-app sales
* Implement StoreKit and Google Play Billing directly per platform
* Sell digital content only through external websites

== Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Use RevenueCat for managing in-app sales", because a single
cross-platform SDK and dashboard handles purchases, subscriptions, receipt
validation, and customer management for both stores. Positive consequences: one
integration point for StoreKit and Google Play Billing, unified subscription and
entitlement management, and less store-specific code to maintain. Negative
consequences: reliance on a third-party service and an additional dependency in
the mobile apps.

RevenueCat is the best option, because it avoids maintaining StoreKit and Google
Play Billing integrations and subscription logic per platform. If "Implement
StoreKit and Google Play Billing directly" was chosen, we would own receipt
validation, renewal handling, and store-specific edge cases. If "Sell digital
content only through external websites" was chosen, in-app purchases would be
unavailable and the apps would not comply with store purchase requirements.
