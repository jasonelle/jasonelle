== Context and Problem Statement

Jasonelle documents decisions implicitly across issues, changelogs, and source
code, which makes it hard to know why a design choice was made. We want a
lightweight, versioned way to record important decisions and their rationale so
future contributors can understand the reasoning.

== Considered Options

* Use Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) following the MADR format.
* Keep documenting decisions ad hoc in issue comments and pull requests.
* Use a standalone wiki outside the repository.

== Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Use Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) following the MADR
format", because ADRs are lightweight, versioned alongside the code in this
repository, and published automatically with the Antora docs. Positive
consequences: decisions are easy to find, reviewable in pull requests, and
searchable in the published site. Negative consequences: a small authoring
overhead is required to keep records up to date.

ADR is the best option, because the other options scatter knowledge outside the
codebase or outside review. If "Keep documenting decisions ad hoc in issue
comments" was chosen, decisions would remain hard to discover. If "Use a
standalone wiki" was chosen, the wiki would drift out of sync with the code.
